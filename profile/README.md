# LTS Engineering

**Embedded & Critical Systems Consulting**

LTS Engineering brings technical expertise and project leadership to embedded, real-time, and IoT systems. 
We support aerospace and defense prime contractors, as well as logistics operators, through the design, development, and validation of critical systems.

## About us 🙋‍♀️
### What we do

- **Embedded systems** — dependable software and hardware integration for demanding applications
- **Real-time systems** — engineering across RTOS platforms and tight timing, memory, and processing constraints
- **IoT** — firmware, connectivity, communication protocols, and device security
- **Technical leadership** — engineering team leadership, technical management, and Agile coaching

### Industries

We work across **aerospace**, **defense**, and **logistics**, where reliable systems and effective delivery matter.

### Experience

Selected experience includes space and satellite systems, lunar lander software, logistics tracking products, and naval systems. LTS draws on trusted partners and experience leading multicultural teams to support customer goals.
